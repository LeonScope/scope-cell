# Third-party model attribution

The robot shown in the screenshots and video uses the original UR5e graphical meshes and nominal kinematic description from **Universal Robots and contributors**.

- Source: [Universal Robots ROS 2 Description](https://github.com/UniversalRobots/Universal_Robots_ROS2_Description/tree/d4042c1f5123c86645cc5dbd650b4f461f697ee9).
- Pinned revision: `d4042c1f5123c86645cc5dbd650b4f461f697ee9`.
- Model: UR5e; seven graphical meshes. The meshes themselves are not distributed in this presentation repository.
- The upstream README assigns BSD-3-Clause to this UR5e material. Its separately stated restrictions for certain other robot models do not apply to the UR5e files used here.
- The gripper, conveyor, camera and bins are simplified components created for Scope Cell, not vendor product models.
